## Phase 1.2 -- the MAC tested the wrong geometry (fixed), and the near volume is the tree

**Defect.** The walk tested `(r_A + r_B)^2 <= theta^2 d^2` with bounding-box centres and half-diagonals while the
real-basis expansions (P2M/M2M/M2L/L2L/L2P) are about the centres of mass. About the COMs the accepted far pairs
reached convergence ratios of 1.56 at theta 0.6 (0.6 % of pairs >= 1), 2.38 at 0.8 (8.6 %), 4.84 at 1.0 (35 %):
divergent expansions. Symptoms: aggL2 flat in p at theta 0.8 (5.3e-3 / 4.1e-3 / 3.5e-3 for p 4/5/6) and RISING at
theta 1.0 (1.7e-2 / 1.8e-2 / 3.7e-2). Fix: `jaccpot/runtime/_mac_geometry.py` (`JACCPOT_STATIC_STRICT_FUSED_MAC_GEOMETRY=com`,
exact COM radii by default; the child-sphere bound costs 2.7x far pairs). After: theta 0.8 p4/5/6 = 2.06e-3 / 1.02e-3 /
5.9e-4 at 47.9 / 56.4 / 61.2 ms (monotone), theta 0.6 p6 8.3e-5 at 85 ms. Front unchanged in time: near share still
0.12 N at theta 0.8 (jz-fmm 0.0116 N).

**Why the volume:** 341 of 3125 radix leaves with radius > 4x median (11 % of particles) sit in 56 % of the direct
volume -- 64-particle Morton BUCKETS in the r ~ 3-5 shell straddle several cells; the top 1 % of leaves are neighbours
of every row. jz-tree's leaves are Morton CELLS. The same walk (COM, exact radii) on cell leaves
(`probe_tree_volume.py`, Karras radix over the cell codes):

| leaves | direct share th0.8 | far pairs (dir) th0.8 | direct th0.6 | far th0.6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| radix buckets 64 (3125) | 0.1225 N | 565k | 0.236 N | 1.02M |
| radix buckets 32 (6250) | 0.061 N | 1.17M | 0.121 N | 2.35M |
| **cells 64 (10956, occ 18.3)** | **0.0052 N** | 690k | 0.0129 N | 1.58M |
| cells 32 (20701, occ 9.7) | 0.0019 N | 1.21M | 0.0051 N | 2.78M |
| cells 16 (40972, occ 4.9) | 0.0008 N | 2.08M | 0.0018 N | 4.77M |

Cells inside the fused lane's fixed balanced bucket structure give the same near volume but 2.3x the far pairs (1.59M),
so the Karras structure over the cells is the one built. Padding leaves (one centre, radius 0) MUST be dead in the walk:
unmasked they made 30M near edges. yggdrax's "adaptive octree" mode makes buckets, not cells.
